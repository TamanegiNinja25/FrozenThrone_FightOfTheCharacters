# v0.10.1 — phone performance

## What was measured (Infinix NOTE 30, crowded Classic match, detailed creep models, 90% resolution, 2 shadow cascades)
- **v0.10.0 on a cool phone (battery 34 °C):** 42.6 FPS average, 35.1 with 250+ units.
  - GPU 18.4 ms per frame; 508k triangles and 495 draw calls per frame.
  - The ground mesh was only ~42k of those triangles.
- **Shadows off (same build):** 46.3 FPS average, 42.3 with 250+ units; 141k triangles, 154 draw calls.
  - So the shadow passes drew about two-thirds of all triangles.
- **Where the shadow triangles came from:**
  - The Phase 10 monster models cast shadows. The older simple creep shapes never did; that was an existing rule to keep pile-ups cheap.
  - Every creep was drawn again in each cascade.
  - The scenery was one merged mesh spanning the map, so each cascade redrew all of it. That included the mountains and forest outside the walls (41k triangles), whose shadows fall outside the field.

## What changed
- **Ground mesh:**
  - Built in 2 × 2 m blocks. A flat block of one colour is one quad.
  - Blocks with slopes, cliff edges or colour changes keep the fine 0.5 m grid.
  - A flat block next to a fine one becomes a small fan through that side's grid vertices, so neighbours share edges exactly and no cracks appear.
  - Size: 11.5k triangles and 7.8k shared vertices, down from 42.5k triangles and ~127k unshared vertices.
  - A test checks that every vertex sits on the terrain height and that the count stays under 15,000.
- **First attempt:** merged large flat patches (up to 16 m) into long, thin fans through every border vertex.
  - Its single run came out at 19 FPS. It was replaced by the block layout.
  - That reading was most likely the warm-phone collapse described below, not the fans themselves.
- **Crowd creeps** (neutral, non-boss) cast no shadow and aren't drawn beyond 48 m, the same as the simple shapes. Bosses and heroes' creatures keep their shadows.
- **Scenery:** split into the field props (castles, towers, banners, camps, rocks, duel trees), which keep their shadows, and the backdrop (mountains, hills, forest), which casts none.

## Result: interleaved runs at the same phone temperature (37–38 °C)

| Build | Average FPS | With 250+ units | GPU per frame | Triangles | Draw calls |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| v0.10.1 (run 1) | 43.1 | 40.0 | 17.0 ms | 251k | 264 |
| v0.10.0 | 19.0 | 18.9 | 39.8 ms | 531k | 528 |
| v0.10.1 (run 2) | 38.4 | 35.1 | 21.5 ms | 242k | 235 |

- On a warm phone, v0.10.0's heavier frames drop the GPU into a much slower state, so frame time doubles. The same build ran at 42.6 when cool.
- v0.10.1 roughly halves the triangles and draw calls and stayed at 38–43 FPS in the same conditions.
- No script errors in any run.
- Single runs mislead by the phone's temperature; comparisons need to be interleaved.
