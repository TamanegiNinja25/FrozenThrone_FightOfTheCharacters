# v0.12.0 — items, icons, app icon, cliff rims

## App icon
- **The supplied picture:** fan art of two characters from a copyrighted anime series, with the fan artist's signature. It was not used, because the repository and APK are public.
- **The original replacement**, in the picture's spirit:
  - two of the game's own heroes, Oren the Windcutter and Nyx the Shade Dancer, squaring off;
  - black and white, in front of grey bamboo-like stalks;
  - the title in blood red (Dancing Script), with a red inner frame.
- `tools/render_icon.gd` renders it, with the Android adaptive layers.

- **Descriptions:** every artifact names its components ("Made from: …"). Every part lists what it builds into ("Builds into: …").
- **Shop:** an artifact's detail lists each component with its price and ticks the ones the hero carries; a part's detail shows what it builds into.
- **Balance test:** passes. New components buy power at 0.80–0.96 per gold; the new recipes are worth 20–40% more than their parts.
- **Bots:** their builds are unchanged.

## Icons
- **Source:** all 190 item and skill icons are re-rendered from game-icons.net glyphs. That's 152 icons by Lorc, Delapouite, Carl Olsen, Sbed, Lucas, Skoll, Cathelineau and DarkZaitzev, under CC BY 3.0, from the project's official GitHub repository.
- **Style:** each glyph is recoloured and framed by `tools/render_icons.gd`. Items are rounded squares tinted by shop; skills are circles tinted by the hero's realm.
- **Licence and credit:** the licence file sits beside the glyphs. `CREDITS.md` and the Credits screen credit the authors. The old code-drawn icon generators no longer run.

## High ground
- A pale rim runs along every cliff top and pit edge, on the high side, so high ground reads at a glance from the camera.
- It's one unshaded mesh with no shadow, built from the same segments as the invisible cliff walls.
