# v0.11.2 — Lys and Corin tuned in their skills

## Why skills, not stats
- **Lys:** in the v0.11.1 confirmation run she still won 75% of team fights, with the fewest deaths (2.8 a match), despite a 0.92 HP multiplier.
  - Her strength was team-fight control and sustain:
    - a piercing wave;
    - a pull that healed for 100% of its damage;
    - a 45% slowing whirlpool;
    - a long knockback.
  - In the 1v1 duel model she is the weakest hero (0.60 against an equal bruiser at level 20), so cutting HP hurt the wrong thing.
- **Corin:** he still lost (29%) after a 1.12 HP / 1.10 damage boost. He dealt the least damage of any hero, and his kit had little damage and no sustain for a melee bruiser.

## Changes
| Hero | Skill | Before | After |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lys | Tide Wave | 125 damage | 110 |
| Lys | Undertow | heals 100% of damage | heals 50% |
| Lys | Whirlpool | 45% slow | 35% slow |
| Lys | Moon Tide | knockback 14 | knockback 10 |
| Lys | base HP multiplier | ×0.92 | removed (×1.0) |
| Corin | Spirit Shot | 120 damage, 7 s cooldown | 150, 6 s |
| Corin | Spirit Fist | 110, 1 s stun, 9 s cooldown | 150, 1 s stun, heals a third, 8 s |
| Corin | Fighting Spirit | +50% attack speed, 15% less damage, 5 s, 14 s cooldown | +60%, 20% less, 6 s, 13 s |
| Corin | Spirit Cannon | 420 | 480 |
| Corin | base HP/damage multipliers | ×1.12 / ×1.10 | kept |

## Checks
- The item and damage balance test passes. Lys's duel ratio at level 20 stays 0.60, because restoring her HP offsets the Tide Wave trim.
