# Hero balance after a full-roster Mobile run and the first Classic run

## The runs (v0.12.0 build)
- **Mobile 3v3:** 48 matches in three parallel copies, about 12 games per hero, so ±14% is noise.
- **Classic 4v4:** 16 matches in two copies, the first ever. Each was capped at 12 minutes, with the kill leader winning if 100 kills wasn't reached. That gives 2–9 games per hero, so single heroes are very noisy.
- **Classic pacing:** matches rarely reached 100 kills within 12 minutes (scores from 31–96 to 48–53). That fits the map's no-time-limit Classic.

## Signals acted on (large in Mobile, or the same direction in both modes)
| Hero | Mobile win % (games) | Classic win % (games) | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mire | 75% (12) | 75% (4) | strong in both |
| Morrow | 71% (14) | 100% (3) | strong in both |
| Sable | 64% (11) | 100% (6) | strong in both |
| Lys | 20% (10) | 33% (6) | the v0.11.2 Undertow cut overshot |
| Corin | 22% (9) | 33% (6) | weak again without his stat boost |
| Pyre | 25% (12) | 50% (2) | the most deaths in Mobile, 9.1 a match |
| Seraphine | 27% (11) | 25% (4) | weak in both |
| Ryn | 35% (20) | 100% (2) | the most damage in Mobile (13k/min) but 8.2 deaths |

Everyone else won 30–64% in Mobile.
