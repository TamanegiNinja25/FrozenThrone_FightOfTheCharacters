# v0.11.3 — the real cause of Corin's and Kael's low damage

## Diagnosis
- **The pattern:** Kael and Corin dealt the least damage per minute of all 24 heroes in the 48-match run (4.4k and 3.7k against ~7.7k). Stat multipliers (v0.11.1) and skill buffs (v0.11.2) barely moved it.
- **The common factor:** they are the only two **Bruisers whose main attribute is agility**. Every other Bruiser and Giant has strength.
- **The cause:** bots buy by role, and the Bruiser build is strength items (Titan's Belt, twice, fused with the axe and shield). Only the main attribute adds attack damage, so those items gave these two almost nothing.
- So the problem was in the bots' shopping, not in the heroes' design.

## Fix
- **New bot build** for agility Bruisers, in this order:
  - Swiftstride Boots;
  - Warbreaker Axe;
  - capture orb;
  - Homeward Scroll;
  - signature item;
  - Titan's Belt (fuses with the axe into the Labyrinth Cleaver);
  - Ember Idol (fuses with the boots into Victor's Medal, +25 to every attribute);
  - Tower Shield.

  It fits 6 slots once the recipes fuse.
- `ItemCatalog.bot_build(role, primary)` picks it for a Bruiser with agility as the main attribute. A test checks it.
- **Balance sim:** a third argument puts one hero in every match (`-- 6 0 corin`).

## Measurement (24 matches: 12 with Corin, 12 with Kael, plus incidental appearances)

| Hero | Win % (games) | Damage/min | Deaths/match | Before the fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Corin | 64% (14) | 8.2k | 3.9 | 3.5k damage/min, 3.7–7.3 deaths |
| Kael | 36% (14) | 6.6k | 3.8 | 4.4–5.0k damage/min |
| The other 22 heroes (average) | — | 7.7k | 5.3 | — |

- **Corin** now deals more than an average hero, because three fixes were stacked: stat multipliers, skill buffs and the right build. His v0.11.1 stat multipliers (HP ×1.12, damage ×1.10) were removed; his v0.11.2 skill changes stay.
- **Kael** is still a little below average damage, so he keeps his multipliers (HP ×1.05, damage ×1.12). His 36% is within the noise of 14 games.
