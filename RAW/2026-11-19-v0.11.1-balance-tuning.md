# v0.11.1 — hero balance after a 48-match bot run

## The run
- **Setup:** 48 all-bot Mobile (3v3) matches at 8× speed on v0.11.0, rotating all 24 heroes. Three copies ran in parallel, 16 matches each. Each hero played 7–16 matches (about 12 on average); 3 matches ended in a tie.
- **Noise:** at that sample size a win rate is uncertain by about ±14%, so only the extremes count.
- **Too strong:**
  - Thane 73% (11 matches);
  - Lys 69% (13);
  - Velka 67% (12; 4.5 kills a match).
- **Too weak:**
  - Dunmar 27% (15; low damage, 5.3k a minute);
  - Nyx 27% (11; low damage, 5.2k a minute, 6.5 deaths);
  - Kael 29% (14; low damage, 4.4k a minute);
  - Corin 38% (13; the lowest damage, 3.7k a minute, and the most deaths, 7.3 a match).
- **Everyone else** won 31–64%.
- **Lys:** she was the weakest hero in the 1v1 duel model at level 20 (0.6 against an equal bruiser), yet among the strongest in 3v3. Her area skills win team fights, which a one-on-one model can't capture.

## The tuning
A table in `Roster.all()` multiplies each listed hero's base HP and attack damage. Skills and items are unchanged.

| Hero | HP | Attack damage | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thane | ×0.88 | ×0.97 | too strong; a Giant, so mostly HP |
| Lys | ×0.92 | — | too strong in team fights; the gentlest lever |
| Velka | ×0.95 | ×0.93 | too strong, with a high kill rate |
| Dunmar | ×1.05 | ×1.12 | low damage |
| Nyx | ×1.12 | ×1.08 | dies often, low damage |
| Kael | ×1.05 | ×1.12 | low damage |
| Corin | ×1.12 | ×1.10 | most deaths, lowest damage |

## Checks
- The item and damage balance test still passes: mirror fights last 2.6–13.5 s.
- Duels against an equal bruiser:

  | Level | Duel ratio range |
  |---|---|
  | 10 | 0.76–1.02 |
  | 20 | 0.60–1.07 |
  | 30 | 0.91–1.26 |


## Confirmation run (24 matches on the tuned build)
- **Sample:** 3–9 games per hero, so individual win rates are very noisy. Oren won 2 of 2; Pyre lost 6 of 6 and Morrow 4 of 4. Damage per minute and deaths per match are steadier signals.
- **Results:**

  | Hero | Win % before → after (games) | Damage/min before → after | Deaths/match before → after |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Thane | 73% (11) → 40% (5) | 8.0k → 7.5k | 3.3 → 4.2 |
  | Velka | 67% (12) → 33% (3) | 9.2k → 7.4k | 6.8 → 5.3 |
  | Dunmar | 27% (15) → 43% (7) | 5.3k → 5.8k | 4.8 → 3.7 |
  | Nyx | 27% (11) → 56% (9) | 5.2k → 8.4k | 6.5 → 4.1 |
  | Kael | 29% (14) → 44% (9) | 4.4k → 5.0k | 5.4 → 4.4 |
  | Corin | 38% (13) → 29% (7) | 3.7k → 4.2k | 7.3 → 5.4 |
  | Lys | 69% (13) → 75% (8) | 8.2k → 7.1k | 3.9 → 2.8 |

- **Moved as intended:** Thane and Velka toned down; Dunmar, Nyx and Kael stronger.
- **Not resolved:**
  - **Lys** still wins most team fights. Cutting HP doesn't touch the area skills that carry her, so a skill trim is the next lever.
  - **Corin** dies less but still loses. His damage output is the issue.
- **Next:** a longer run (48+ matches), then skill-level tuning for Lys and Corin.
