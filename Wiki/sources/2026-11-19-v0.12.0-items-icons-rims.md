# v0.12.0 — items, icons, app icon, cliff rims
- **Items:** four new components and eight new recipes (15 in all), including a three-part banner, two upgrades of map artifacts and a new lifesteal stat. Artifacts name their components, parts name what they build into, and the shop lists components with prices.
- **Icons:** all 190 are re-rendered from game-icons.net glyphs (CC BY 3.0).
- **App icon:** an original black-and-white duel of two heroes; the supplied fan-art picture was declined.
- **High ground:** pale rims mark every cliff top.

## Key points
- **New components:**
  - Quickfang Gauntlets (+40% attack speed, 2,500);
  - Bloodfang Charm (12% lifesteal, 3,000);
  - Vigor Band (+40 HP/s, 2,500);
  - Arcane Focus (+10 int, 10% faster skills, 3,000).
- **New recipes:**
  - Bloodreaver, Stormstrike Gauntlets, Lifewell Plate, Archmage Crown, Phantom Edge;
  - Warlord's Banner (three parts);
  - upgrades: Kingsguard Aegis (Golden Bulwark + Vigor Band) and Worldsplitter (Labyrinth Cleaver + Bloodfang Charm).
- **Balance test:** passes; new recipes are worth 20–40% more than their parts. Bots' builds are unchanged.
- **Icons:**
  - 152 glyphs by Lorc, Delapouite and six other contributors, recoloured and framed by `tools/render_icons.gd`;
  - items tinted by shop, skills by realm;
  - credited in `CREDITS.md` and on the Credits screen.
- **App icon:** Oren and Nyx in monochrome before grey stalks, the title in blood red. The owner's picture (fan art of copyrighted anime characters, signed by its artist) isn't used.
- **Cliff rims:** one unshaded mesh along the cliff-wall segments, on the high side.
- **Simulator:** a Classic 4v4 mode (12-minute cap, kill leader wins). Full-roster Mobile and Classic runs were in progress at release; tuning follows in v0.12.1.
