# v0.10.1 — phone performance
Measurements and fixes behind v0.10.1. The ground mesh was rebuilt in compact blocks, but most of the cost turned out to be shadows. Crowd creeps' monster models and the scenery backdrop were redrawn in every shadow cascade. Removing those roughly halves triangles and draw calls, and keeps a warm phone out of a GPU slowdown that halved v0.10.0's frame rate.

## Key points
- **v0.10.0, cool phone (34 °C):** 42.6 FPS average, 35.1 with 250+ units; 508k triangles and 495 draw calls per frame. The ground was ~42k of those triangles.
- **Shadows off:** 46.3 FPS average (42.3 with 250+ units); 141k triangles. So shadow passes drew about two-thirds of the triangles.
- **Ground mesh:** 2 × 2 m blocks.
  - A flat block is one quad; slopes keep the 0.5 m grid.
  - Small fans stitch flat blocks to fine ones without cracks.
  - 11.5k triangles, down from 42.5k.
  - A first attempt with long fans over 16 m patches was replaced. Its 19 FPS reading was most likely a warm-phone slowdown, not the fans.
- **Crowd creeps:** no shadows and a 48 m draw distance, matching the older simple shapes. Bosses and heroes' creatures keep their shadows.
- **Scenery:** the backdrop (mountains, hills, forest; 41k triangles) casts no shadow; field props still do.
- **Interleaved runs at 37–38 °C:**

  | Build | Average FPS | With 250+ units | GPU frame | Triangles |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | v0.10.0 | 19.0 | 18.9 | 39.8 ms | 531k |
  | v0.10.1 | 38.4–43.1 | 35.1–40.0 | 17–21.5 ms | ~245k |

- **Method:** single runs mislead because of phone temperature; comparisons must be interleaved.
