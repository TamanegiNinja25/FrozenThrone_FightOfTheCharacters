# Hero balance after a full-roster Mobile run and the first Classic run
A 48-match Mobile run and the first 16-match Classic run of v0.12.0 found Mire, Morrow and Sable strong in both modes, and Lys, Corin, Pyre, Seraphine and Ryn weak. Base HP and damage were nudged by 5–10%, and Lys's Undertow heal was set to 70%. The item and damage balance test still passes.

## Key points
- **Samples:**
  - Mobile: about 12 games per hero, so ±14% is noise.
  - Classic: 2–9 games per hero, a 12-minute cap and the kill leader as winner; very noisy.
- **Classic pacing:** matches rarely reach 100 kills within 12 minutes, which fits the map's no-time-limit Classic.
- **Strong:** Mire (75% Mobile, 75% Classic), Morrow (71%, 100%), Sable (64%, 100%).
- **Weak:**
  - Lys 20% (the Undertow cut overshot);
  - Corin 22% (weak again without a boost);
  - Pyre 25% (9.1 deaths a match);
  - Seraphine 27% (weak in both modes);
  - Ryn 35% (the most damage but 8.2 deaths).
- **Changes:**

  | Hero | Change |
  |---|---|
  | Mire | HP ×0.90 |
  | Morrow | HP ×0.92, damage ×0.95 |
  | Sable | damage ×0.94 |
  | Lys | Undertow heals 70% |
  | Corin | HP ×1.06, damage ×1.05 |
  | Pyre | HP ×1.10 |
  | Seraphine | HP ×1.05, damage ×1.08 |
  | Ryn | HP ×1.08 |

- **Check:** item/damage balance test passes; duel ratios 0.60–1.26.
