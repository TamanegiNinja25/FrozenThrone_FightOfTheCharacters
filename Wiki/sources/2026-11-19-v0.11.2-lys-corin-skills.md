# v0.11.2 — Lys and Corin tuned in their skills
The v0.11.1 stat multipliers didn't move Lys (still 75% of team fights) or Corin (still 29%), so v0.11.2 tunes their skills. Lys loses some control and sustain, and gets her HP back. Corin hits harder and gains lifesteal.

## Key points
- **Lys:** her edge was control and sustain, not stats:
  - a piercing wave;
  - a pull that healed 100%;
  - a 45% slow;
  - a long knockback.
  She is the weakest 1v1 hero, so the HP cut was removed.
- **Lys changes:**
  - Tide Wave 125 → 110;
  - Undertow heals 50% (was 100%);
  - Whirlpool slow 35% (was 45%);
  - Moon Tide knockback 10 (was 14).
- **Corin:** the least damage of any hero, and no sustain.
- **Corin changes:**
  - Spirit Shot 150 every 6 s (was 120 / 7 s);
  - Spirit Fist 150 with a third healed back, every 8 s (was 110 / 9 s);
  - Fighting Spirit +60% attack speed, 20% less damage, 6 s (was +50% / 15% / 5 s);
  - Spirit Cannon 480 (was 420).
- **Checks:** item/damage balance test passes; Lys's level-20 duel stays 0.60.

- **Bot run on this build (24 matches):**
  - Lys 57% (7 games), deaths back to 4.0 a match: near the middle.
  - Corin dies half as often (3.7) with more kills, but played only 3 games. His damage per minute stays the lowest, which suggests the bots play his melee kit poorly.
