# v0.11.3 — the real cause of Corin's and Kael's low damage
Corin and Kael, the only agility Bruisers, dealt the least damage because bots bought them strength items, which don't raise an agility hero's attack damage. A dedicated bot build more than doubled Corin's damage, so his stat multipliers were removed.

## Key points
- **Cause:** the Bruiser bot build is strength items; only the main attribute adds attack damage. Kael and Corin are the only Bruisers with agility as their main attribute.
- **Fix:** an agility-Bruiser build: boots first, then Victor's Medal (boots + idol) and the Labyrinth Cleaver. `bot_build(role, primary)` picks it.
- **Result (24 matches focused on them):**
  - Corin 64%, 8.2k damage/min (was 3.5k);
  - Kael 36%, 6.6k (was 4.4–5.0k);
  - the other heroes average 7.7k.
- **Follow-up:** Corin's v0.11.1 stat multipliers were removed (three stacked fixes overshot); Kael keeps his.
- **Lesson:** when stat and skill changes don't move a hero, check how the bots equip him.
