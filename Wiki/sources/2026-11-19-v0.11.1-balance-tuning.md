# v0.11.1 — hero balance after a 48-match bot run
A 48-match all-bot run of v0.11.0 found three heroes winning too often and four losing too often. A tuning table nudges their base HP and attack damage by 3–12%, and the item and damage balance test still passes.

## Key points
- **The run:** 48 Mobile matches (three parallel runs of 16); about 12 games per hero, so ±14% is noise.
- **Outliers:**
  - Too strong: Thane 73%, Lys 69%, Velka 67%.
  - Too weak: Dunmar 27%, Nyx 27%, Kael 29%, Corin 38% (lowest damage, most deaths).
- **Lys:** weakest in the 1v1 duel model, yet strong in 3v3. Area skills win team fights, which a one-on-one model can't see.
- **Tuning (base HP and attack damage multipliers):**

  | Hero | HP | Attack damage |
  |---|---|---|
  | Thane | 0.88 | 0.97 |
  | Lys | 0.92 | — |
  | Velka | 0.95 | 0.93 |
  | Dunmar | 1.05 | 1.12 |
  | Nyx | 1.12 | 1.08 |
  | Kael | 1.05 | 1.12 |
  | Corin | 1.12 | 1.10 |

- **Checks:** item/damage balance test passes; duel ratios 0.60–1.26.

- **Confirmation run (24 matches; 3–9 games per hero, noisy):**
  - Thane 73 → 40%, Velka 67 → 33%, Dunmar 27 → 43%, Nyx 27 → 56%, Kael 29 → 44%.
  - Corin 38 → 29% but dies less (7.3 → 5.4).
  - Lys 69 → 75%: unchanged, because her strength is her area skills.
  - Next: skill-level tuning for Lys and Corin.
